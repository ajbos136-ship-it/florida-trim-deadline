# The 25-day deadline on your Florida TRIM notice

A free, sourced explainer on the deadline that runs from your Florida TRIM notice, for
Sumter, Lake and Marion counties.

**Read it: https://ajbos136-ship-it.github.io/florida-trim-deadline/**

Every claim on the page names the statute it came from and was read against the Florida
Statutes directly, not against other guides. Sources cited: Fla. Stat. §§ 192.042, 193.011,
193.155(1), 194.011, 194.013, 194.014, 194.035, 194.301, and Florida Department of Revenue
form DR-486.

Three things it covers that short guides usually get wrong:

- The clock runs from the **mailing** of the notice, not from receipt (§ 194.011(3)(d)).
- There are **two** deadlines, 25 days for value and 30 days for a denied exemption, on two
  different notices.
- § 194.013 caps the filing fee at **$50** per parcel and lets each county set its own under
  that ceiling. The figure $15 does not appear in that statute at all.
